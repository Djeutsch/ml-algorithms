Decision tree

G = 1 − Σk pk2

Cut the plane with one vertical or horizontal line at a time, always choosing the cut that leaves the two sides purest, measured by Gini impurity G. A 50/50 mix scores one half; a pure box scores zero.

Random forest

ŷ = mode(h1(x), …, hT(x))

Grow many trees, each on a bootstrap resample with random features at each split, then let them vote. Single trees are jagged and disagree; their majority is smoother and steadier.

Distance-based · clustering

K-means

μj = 1⁄|Cj| Σi∈Cj xi

No labels. Drop k centres, assign every point to its nearest centre, move each centre μj to the mean of its points, and repeat until nothing moves. The faint paths trace each centre.

How the sample data was made
Regression line
Sixty points along a straight line with Gaussian noise. Gradient descent starts from a deliberately poor line, like the video's opening frame.
Logistic
Sixty points on one axis; the chance of class 1 follows a sigmoid centred mid-range.
Two moons
Scikit-learn's interleaving half-moons with noise, split into training and held-out test points. Shared by the tree, forest, boosting, KNN, neural-network and logistic comparison.
Blobs
Three Gaussian clusters for k-means. Their true groups are used only to score the result.
Shading
Colour shows the predicted class; stronger shading means a more confident model.
